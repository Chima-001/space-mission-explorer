Attributions and sources

Space Mission Explorer is an educational project. It does not own any of the launch or image data it displays; everything belongs to the organisations below, and every record links back to its source.

Launch data

Launch schedules, statuses, rockets, pads, providers and mission descriptions come from The Space Devs, through their Launch Library 2 API.

  • Endpoints used: /launch/upcoming/ and /launch/previous/, plus /launch/<id>/ for a single mission.
  • No API key is required, and no account is used.
  • Requests are limited, so responses are cached in your browser for 30 minutes.
  • Launch images are hosted and credited by The Space Devs on their own media CDN.

Imagery

The hero images, the featured picture and the related images on mission pages come from the NASA Image and Video Library, which is operated by NASA.

  • Search requests use images-api.nasa.gov/search with media_type=image.
  • Each picture credits its NASA centre, such as JPL, GSFC or KSC, in the caption.
  • Clicking a picture opens the original record on images.nasa.gov.
  • NASA does not endorse this project. NASA content is generally not protected by copyright, but NASA's media guidelines still apply to its use.

Fonts and type

  • Space Grotesk for headings, used under the SIL Open Font License.
  • Inter for body text, used under the SIL Open Font License.
  • Both are served by Google Fonts, with system fonts as a fallback.
